GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A little clarity before you take the next step.

Is the EMI amount guaranteed by a lender?+

No. This calculator gives an estimate. A lender's final EMI depends on the approved amount, rate, tenure, fees and repayment terms.

Does submitting an enquiry mean my loan is approved?+

No. An enquiry is only a request for contact. Approval is decided by the lender after its own checks.

What information should I share here?+

Share only the basic contact details requested. Never submit an OTP, password, card PIN, net-banking credentials or sensitive identity documents in this demo form.

Are there any fees or charges?+

Fees and charges vary by lender and product. Review the lender's official schedule of charges and loan documents before agreeing.
