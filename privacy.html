Privacy notice

Draft template — review before production. Replace this notice with a policy reviewed for your actual business, technology providers, applicable law and data practices.

Information collected

The enquiry form design requests name, phone number, loan interest, approximate loan range, city, preferred callback time and an optional message. In this supplied demo, the form does not transmit or save submitted details.

Purpose and consent

Before production use, explain exactly how enquiry information will be used, who can access it, whether it is shared with lending partners, how long it is retained, and how a person can withdraw consent or request deletion.

Security and contact

Use HTTPS, server-side validation, access controls, retention limits, monitoring and a secure data store. Add the verified legal business name and a monitored privacy contact before launch. Do not request OTPs, passwords, card PINs or online banking credentials.
